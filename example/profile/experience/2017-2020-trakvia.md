# Trakvia, March 2017 to February 2020

Walked shallow on 2026-08-19. One story, one attested number, open questions at the bottom.

## The context

Trakvia built fleet telematics for regional road carriers in Poland and Czechia. Forty-five people, profitable, family-owned, no outside investment. Ada joined as a quality automation engineer, the fourth hire on a team of six engineers, and moved onto the backend team in October 2018 after the tech lead asked her to.

She reported to Piotr Wrona, the tech lead, for the whole three years. Nobody reported to her.

## Story, the test suite nobody could run

**Situation.** The integration suite took fifty-two minutes and failed on the main branch about fourteen times a week without anybody having broken anything. Engineers stopped reading the results, and two real regressions shipped in the same quarter because the red build was assumed to be flaky.

**What I did.** I spent a week tagging every failure by cause instead of fixing them. Four causes covered forty of the fifty-one failures I logged, and three of the four were tests sharing one database fixture. I rewrote the fixture so each test got its own schema, split the suite into a fast path and a slow path, and put the slow path on a nightly run. I argued for the split with Piotr twice, because he wanted the whole suite on every push and I wanted the feedback under ten minutes more than I wanted completeness.

**Result.** The fast path settled at nine minutes. Main branch failures dropped from about fourteen a week to under two, measured on the continuous integration dashboard over the following quarter and pulled by Piotr for the team retrospective in November 2018.

**Reflection.** I fixed the fixture and I never wrote down why the fixture was shared in the first place, so it grew back eighteen months later in a different service.

## What she would do differently

Ask to move to backend a year earlier. She stayed in quality automation past the point where she was learning, because moving felt like asking for something.
