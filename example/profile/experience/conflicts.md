# Conflicts

Places where two sources of her own history disagree. Nothing here is resolved, on purpose. A quiet reconciliation picks one figure and destroys the evidence that there was ever a disagreement, and the disagreement is what she will be asked about.

`method/funnel/materials.md` reads this file before it writes any number, and it resolves to the smaller one with a note.

## The nightly pipeline, runtime against finish time

**Source A.** Her 2024 performance review at Lomvik, written by Jonas Vogt: "cut the nightly pipeline from eleven hours to four".

**Source B.** The scheduler run log she exported in March 2023: median finish time moved from 11:10 to 04:40, and median run duration moved from six hours ten minutes to two hours five minutes.

**What is actually in dispute.** The review is quoting finish times as if they were durations. Eleven and four are the clock times the run ended, not the hours it took. Both sources describe the same improvement and neither is lying. Every version of her resume before September 2026 carried "eleven hours to four", which is the number she would have repeated in a room.

**Standing resolution.** Use the run duration, six hours ten to two hours five, and say it as "just over six hours to just over two". It is the smaller claim and it is the one the log attests. Where the finish time is the point, say "it finished before five in the morning instead of after eleven". Never say eleven hours.

## The client event loss, three figures for one number

**Source A.** The growth team's dashboard, which reported the funnel gap as "about three percent" from 2023 onward.

**Source B.** Her sequence-gap monitor, which measured three point one percent at the start of July 2024 and zero point two percent by the end of September.

**Source C.** A vendor assessment commissioned in May 2024 that put mobile event loss at "approximately five percent".

**What is actually in dispute.** The vendor measured a different thing, probably including events the client never attempted to send, and nobody at Lomvik ever reconciled the two. The five percent figure is the one that got quoted in a company all-hands, so it is the number some former colleagues remember.

**Standing resolution.** Three point one to zero point two, from her own monitor, because it is the only figure she measured and can explain end to end. If somebody who worked there says five, she says where the five came from rather than arguing with it.
