# Voice profile

Extracted from three samples on 2026-08-18: a message to a colleague explaining a pipeline failure, a design note arguing against a second warehouse, and a private end-of-year note she wrote for herself. The cover letter she offered first was set aside, because it was written in the register this profile exists to see past.

## What repeats

- Short sentences. Ten to fourteen words is the normal run, with one long sentence per paragraph and no more.
- She opens with the conclusion and then explains it. All three samples do this, including the private one.
- She quantifies by habit. Every one of the three samples carries at least two figures, and she never rounds a figure she measured herself.
- She names people. Colleagues appear by first name, with what they did, rather than as "the team".
- Her default register is plain and slightly flat. It rises only when she is disagreeing.
- She signals disagreement by restating the other position accurately first, then giving one number against it. She uses this move every time.
- She writes "I decided" and "I was wrong" without softening either one.

## What she never does

- She never uses an exclamation mark, in any of the three samples.
- She never opens with a greeting longer than a name.
- She never describes her own work with an adjective. Not once across nine pages.
- She never uses "leverage", "robust", "seamless" or "passionate".

## Where this profile and the gate disagree

Her design note runs to three paragraphs of context before the recommendation, which is how internal documents are read at her last employer and is not how a cover letter is read. `method/voice.md` wins here and the opening gets cut. The habit is recorded because a profile that agrees with the gate about everything is worth nothing.
