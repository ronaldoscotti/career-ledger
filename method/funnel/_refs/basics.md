# basics

Five questions enter every interview preparation. Not the ones judged likely for this stage, not the ones the posting suggests. These five, every time, whatever the method and whatever the time budget.

On 2026-08-03 a preparation shipped twenty-one cards an hour before a call, with no behavioral basics in it and no card answering any requirement of the posting. Three audits had run over that preparation and none of them caught the gap, because each audit checked whether what was written was correct, and none asked what was missing. An audit of what is present does not substitute for a fixed list of what must be present. That is why this file is a list and not a heuristic.

## The five

| Question | What it is testing | Where the answer comes from | The failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tell me about yourself | Whether the person can select. The room is watching what they choose to say in ninety seconds. | `profile/masters/` for the arc, one story from `profile/experience/` for the proof. | A chronological walk from the first job. The answer has to arrive at why this room, not at the present day. |
| Your strongest contribution | The ceiling of what they have owned, and whether they can claim it without hedging. | The largest owned theme in `method/funnel/_refs/star.md`, with its locked number. | Naming the project instead of the contribution. The room wants the part that was the person. |
| A real weakness | Self-knowledge, and whether they are being handled or being met. | `profile/experience/`, where the limitation is recorded with what it cost. | The disguised strength. See below. |
| A disagreement and what happened | Whether they can be wrong in public, and whether the other person is described fairly. | The conflict theme and the wrong-decision theme. | A story with no real disagreement in it, or one where the person was right the whole time. |
| Where this goes in a few years | Whether the person's direction and the seat point the same way. | `profile/PROFILE.md` for the target, the posting for the seat. | An ambition that reads as wanting a different job than the one being offered. |

For each, the answer is spoken, not read. Run every one of the five through `method/funnel/_refs/spoken-gate.md`.

## The collision rule

The same story must not answer two of the five. A bank projected carelessly collapses onto its best story, and the room hears the same project four times, which reads as a person with one good quarter.

Check for collisions when the preparation is assembled, before it is delivered. Lay the five answers side by side and look at which story each one uses. A collision found during the call costs the second answer, because the person notices mid-sentence that they are repeating themselves.

## The weakness question

This is the one most often answered with a disguised strength, and interviewers have heard every version. "I care too much" ends the useful part of the conversation and leaves the room with nothing except the knowledge that the person was performing.

What works is a real limitation, stated plainly, with what the person is doing about it and what it has already cost them. The cost is what makes it credible. A limitation with no cost attached is a limitation nobody believes.

There is a counter-rule, and it matters more than the rule. Never turn a weakness into the subject of a story told to sell the person. On 2026-08-17 a form answer opened by naming a limitation of the person in order to set up a story with a good ending. The reaction was that the material was meant to sell them, not to make them look stupid. The structure was sound and the arc worked; the subject was wrong.

So the test is about the subject of the paragraph, not its adjectives. If the sentence the reader remembers is about a limitation, the story is wrong and gets replaced. Softening the wording keeps the same paragraph with a quieter failure in it.
